# Sources: High Signal Podcasts

The source index contains public podcast episodes that support at least one
published claim. It is not a general podcast directory.

For current structured data, call
`GET https://api.podcasts.highsignal.app/api/sources`. Use
`GET /api/sources/{id}` to retrieve one episode and its published claims.

Keep source links and timestamps attached when they are present. Source coverage
is intentionally limited to records that clear the evidence gate.
