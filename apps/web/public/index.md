# What notable people actually said—without listening to every episode

High Signal Podcasts is a public evidence index of attributable claims,
recommendations, books, and tools from notable podcast guests. The unit is the
claim, not the episode.

Every published claim keeps the speaker, exact excerpt, date, and source trail
attached. Missing or low-confidence evidence stays unpublished instead of being
filled in.

## Use it when

- You need to verify what a person said on a public podcast.
- You want source-linked tools, books, or products a guest recommended or uses.
- You want to search published claims by person, topic, type, or date.
