# Methodology — High Signal Podcasts

High Signal Podcasts indexes public statements as claims. A published record has
a speaker, an assertion, a verbatim excerpt found in the stored transcript
segment, a date, and primary source evidence. A source timestamp is included
when the source supports it.

Publisher or YouTube captions are ingested and candidate claims and references
are reviewed against their evidence. Missing, unknown, or low-confidence data
stays unpublished. The product prefers an explicit gap over an invented answer.

Agents should preserve attribution, quotes, dates, evidence links, and the
difference between recommendation roles such as `recommends` and `uses`.
