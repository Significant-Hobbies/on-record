# About: High Signal Podcasts

High Signal Podcasts is a High Signal project for finding attributable ideas and
recommendations in long public conversations. It indexes the claim rather than
the episode while preserving the speaker, verbatim excerpt, date, and source
trail.

The public-beta corpus is deliberately bounded. Unknown speakers, unsupported
interpretations, and low-confidence evidence stay unpublished.

- Methodology: https://podcasts.highsignal.app/methodology
- Source code: https://github.com/Significant-Hobbies/on-record
